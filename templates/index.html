About FA Events

Rooted in Kanyakumari District, styled for every celebration

Based in Parvathipuram near Nagercoil, FA Events has decorated weddings, receptions, engagements and birthdays across Kanyakumari District. We work closely with each family to design decorations that match the venue, the budget and the occasion — from a small home function to a full wedding hall.

Every project is planned on-site, built by our own decoration team, and finished on time so you can focus on the celebration, not the setup.

  • Free on-site consultation before every wedding and event booking
  • Custom flower, balloon, cloth and stage decoration design
  • Serving Parvathipuram, Nagercoil, Colachel, Marthandam, Thuckalay and across Kanyakumari District
  • Direct WhatsApp support for quotes, dates and design references
  • Photo and video gallery updated after every event
